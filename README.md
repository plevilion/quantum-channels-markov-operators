# Quantum Channels as Noncommutative Markov Operators

**Bachelor's Thesis — University of Oslo, Spring 2026**

This thesis studies quantum channels as noncommutative analogues of classical
Markov operators. Starting from Markov kernels and their associated operators
on spaces of measurable functions, it develops their positivity, normality,
duality, and integral representations.

The thesis then moves to the noncommutative setting, describing quantum
channels as normal completely positive unital maps on operator algebras and
relating their Kraus representations to the classical theory.

In particular, restricting suitable quantum channels to diagonal operators
recovers classical Markov operators associated with stochastic matrices and
Markov kernels, illustrating how classical stochastic dynamics arise as
commutative instances of quantum probabilistic structures.
